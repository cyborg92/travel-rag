# SkyTrail Airways Baggage Policy

> **Training notice:** This is a fictional policy created for a synthetic TravelAssist RAG dataset. It does not represent a real airline.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to tickets marketed and operated by SkyTrail Airways. For codeshare journeys, the operating carrier's baggage rules may apply to individual sectors.

## 2. Cabin baggage

| Cabin | Main cabin bag | Personal item | Maximum main-bag dimensions |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Economy Lite | 1 item, 7 kg | 1 item, 3 kg | 55 x 35 x 25 cm |
| Economy Flex | 1 item, 8 kg | 1 item, 3 kg | 55 x 35 x 25 cm |
| Business | 2 items, 8 kg each | 1 item, 3 kg | 55 x 35 x 25 cm per item |

A personal item must fit under the seat. Examples include a laptop bag, handbag, or small backpack.

### Cabin-baggage exceptions

1. Infants without a separate seat do not receive a cabin-bag allowance, but one small baby-care bag up to 5 kg is permitted.
2. Essential mobility aids are carried without charge and do not count toward the allowance.
3. Cabin bags may be moved to the aircraft hold when overhead space is unavailable.
4. Batteries and restricted articles must comply with the dangerous-goods rules published at check-in.

## 3. Checked baggage

| Journey type | Economy Lite | Economy Flex | Business |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Domestic | 15 kg | 20 kg | 30 kg |
| Short-haul international | 20 kg | 25 kg | 35 kg |
| Long-haul international | 23 kg | 30 kg | 40 kg |

Unless the ticket explicitly uses a piece concept, the allowance is based on total weight. No single checked bag may weigh more than 32 kg.

### Size limit

The combined length, width, and height of one checked bag must not exceed **158 cm**. Oversized baggage requires advance acceptance and may attract a handling fee.

## 4. Special items

- One collapsible stroller is accepted free of charge for an infant or child.
- Sports equipment counts toward the checked allowance unless a separate sports-equipment package was purchased.
- Musical instruments may travel as checked baggage, cabin baggage, or on a separately purchased seat, subject to size and safety requirements.
- A wheelchair or other mobility aid is accepted without charge, but advance notice is recommended.

## 5. Excess baggage

Excess baggage is charged by journey type and the amount above the included allowance. Prepaid baggage must be purchased at least 6 hours before scheduled departure. Airport rates may be higher than prepaid rates.

## 6. Through journeys and codeshares

Where a booking contains multiple operating carriers, the baggage allowance displayed on the ticket or booking confirmation takes priority. If the booking confirmation is unclear, the passenger must be directed to an agent instead of being given an assumed allowance.

## 7. Delayed, damaged, or missing baggage

A passenger should report delayed or damaged baggage before leaving the arrival area and retain the baggage tag and report reference. Reimbursement is not automatic and depends on review of receipts, journey details, and applicable liability limits.

## 8. Examples

- A Domestic Economy Lite passenger with a 17 kg checked bag is 2 kg over the included allowance.
- A Long-haul Economy Flex passenger may check multiple bags totaling 30 kg, but no individual bag may exceed 32 kg.
- A Business passenger on a codeshare must follow the allowance shown on the ticket when it differs from this general policy.
